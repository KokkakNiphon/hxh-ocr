# Blog images

All files here are generated by `scripts/blog/make_assets.py` (re-run it rather than editing them). `pipeline/` is one example page, `web/examples/page1.jpg` (a generated page from `data/pages_v2/val`), read by exactly the browser pipeline in node (`scripts/blog/pipeline_assets.mjs` runs `web/ocr.js` `readPage` on `web/hxh_infer.wasm` with `web/models/{det,model,model_v}.hxhm` and `det.json`'s params, pad and `min_conf`): `01_page` is the input, `02_letterbox` the 640 × 640 detector input copied from wasm memory after `hxh_det_letterbox` (page top-left, white padding, so give it a CSS border), `03_kernel` the int8 text-kernel channel as sigmoid probability in #2340d8 over a faded letterbox, `04_orient` the orientation channel where there is text (#2340d8 horizontal, #7a4fd6 vertical), `05_boxes` the page at 2x with the 12 lines the app shows (numbered in reading order; `badge` in `pipeline.json`), and `crop_N.webp` the reader's exact 48 × 768 input strip for line N (lossless; vertical columns turned on their side; use `image-rendering: pixelated` above 1x), described in `crops.json`, with timings, readings, confidences and ground truth in `pipeline.json`. `synth/` shows the training data, each image with a JSON listing its samples, labels, romaji and pixel boxes: `lines_v4` (25 horizontal `data/synth_v4/val` lines, 64 px rows meant for 48 CSS px), `generator_evolution` (one row each from `data/synth_v1`-`v4` val), `pages_v2` (six `data/pages_v2/val` pages with their ground-truth quads, horizontal blue, vertical purple) and `vertical` (five `synth_v4` vertical columns upright, then turned 90° counter-clockwise as the vertical reader sees them); grid gaps are transparent.

`deep/v6_pages.webp` and `deep/v6_lines_{en,th,ja}.webp` (the deep dive's Appendix E, readers v6) come from `scripts/blog/v6_images.py`: two `pages_v6` val pages cut from `previews/pages_v6.png` (the contact sheet's boxes: labelled Hunter lines green, unlabelled foreign text and reading guides orange), and ten `data/lines_v6_digits/val` lines with numbers, drawn at random (seed 7), with what they say and the int8 readings of the deployed Accurate reader and its v6 version through `scripts/lines_int8.mjs` (the app's `readCrop`); wrong characters red, dropped ones faint and struck through. Only the column heads differ between the three languages. `deep/v6_lines.json` lists the lines and the readings of all four readers.
